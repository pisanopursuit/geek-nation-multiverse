# Geek Nation Multiverse

> **The Permanent Online Convention**

Geek Nation Multiverse is an online convention platform that connects fans, creators, artists, vendors, companies, brands, and communities through a shared multiverse of fandoms.

## Features

- 🌌 Universes
- 🎨 Artist Alley
- 🛍 Booth Marketplace
- 📅 Panels & Events
- 🎓 Multiverse Academy
- 🧸 Collector Marketplace
- 🏢 Companies & Brands
- 🛡 Administration & Moderation
- ⚙️ Platform Settings
