# Geek Nation Multiverse Design Principles

## Mission

Geek Nation Multiverse is a platform connecting fans, creators, collectors, educators, artists, and vendors through shared communities, events, commerce, and collaboration.

## Core Experience

Geek Nation Multiverse should feel like walking through the halls of a major Comic-Con:

- Huge
- Busy
- Colorful
- Exciting
- Welcoming
- Discovery-driven

## Principles

### 1. Discovery First

Every major section should feature compelling content and include a clear invitation to explore more.

Examples:

- Explore Universes →
- Explore Artists →
- Explore Booths →
- Explore Panels & Events →
- Explore the Academy →
- Explore Collectibles →
- Explore Community →

### 2. No Dead Ends

Every major page should provide meaningful next steps through related content, recommendations, or a clear action.

### 3. Convention Before Marketplace

Geek Nation is an online convention ecosystem. Commerce supports the experience but does not define it.

### 4. Community Before Commerce

Fandom, conversation, creativity, learning, and events should remain central to the platform.

### 5. Every Page Feels Alive

Pages should surface activity such as new, trending, starting soon, live now, recently updated, or most active content.

### 6. Relationships Matter

Universes, artists, booths, events, courses, collectors, brands, companies, and users should connect naturally wherever relationships exist.

### 7. One Platform

Navigation, cards, forms, buttons, statuses, spacing, and interactions should remain consistent across modules.

### 8. Make Members the Heroes

The platform should showcase its artists, vendors, educators, collectors, organizers, creators, and fans.

### 9. Role-Aware Experience

Every signed-in member receives one dashboard framework with widgets selected according to their roles and activity.

### 10. Build to Scale

Components, relationships, permissions, and information architecture should remain understandable as the platform grows.

## Shared Component Standard

Version 10.4 introduces the shared component system in:

- `includes/components.php`
- `assets/design-system-v10.css`
- `design-system.php`

All shared components use the `gnm-` CSS prefix to avoid conflicts while legacy module pages are migrated gradually.
